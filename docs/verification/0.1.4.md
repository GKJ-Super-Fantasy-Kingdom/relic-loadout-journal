The 0.1.3 candidate was approved with a physical controller in the combined Super Fantasy Kingdom 0.8.070 profile. Mono.Cecil matched the approved DLL to a local 0.1.3 rebuild and to 0.1.4 across 18 types and all 98 method signatures and bodies, including locals and exception handlers. Fields, properties, references, resources and non-version managed attributes also matched. The comparison excluded version metadata, MVID and build/debug metadata. The project, manifest and BepInPlugin version were advanced together.

The 0.1.4 package passed ZIP inventory, icon, plugin identity, and copied-save regression checks, including completion deduplication and title-screen navigation across 256 modeled focus states. The Human 1× and Undead 1.5× screenshots were captured on game build 0.8.069.
