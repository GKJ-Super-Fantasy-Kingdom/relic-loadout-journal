# Changelog

## 0.1.1

First public release.

- Inspect held relics directly from a row of in-game icons using a controller or mouse.
- Identify completed runs by their local completion date, kingdom crest, hero and final day.
- Preserve a missing relic's ID when its catalog entry or localized title is unavailable.

## 0.1.0

- Record accepted run completions with the kingdom, hero, final day and held relics.
- Browse local profile history from the title menu and inspect relic icons and descriptions from the installed game.
- Retain unknown relic IDs when a content mod is unavailable.
